YOUR PHOTO
==========
The site uses one photo of you: public/photos/profile.jpg
(.jpeg, .png or .webp also work). It is shown as the round portrait in the hero.
Best: a portrait, face and shoulders, 1050 x 1400 px or larger (3:4 or 4:5), under ~400 KB.
After changing it, rebuild:  npm run build && npm start
